Frequently asked

What's in the free version?

The whole honest coach. Your first goal is free from start to finish: the discovery conversation, the honest calibration, your living plan, evidence reads, and Harvest. Fruition+ adds proactive check-ins that reach out before you ask, deeper reads of your evidence, and room to grow more than one goal at a time. The free tier limits how much, never the honesty.

The coach pushed back on my goal, or said it needs more time. Is that broken?

No, that's the whole point of Fruition. It calibrates your ambition honestly and won't rubber-stamp a timeframe that isn't realistic, or a goal that could harm you. You can adjust your timeframe and ask for a fresh read anytime.

How do I restore a purchase on a new device?

Open the Fruition+ screen and tap Restore purchase. Subscriptions are tied to your Apple Account, so make sure you're signed in with the same one you purchased on.

Can I continue on my iPhone and iPad?

Yes. Your goals and your Orchard sync automatically through your own iCloud, using whichever Apple Account is signed in on each device. Make sure iCloud is enabled on both.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us: open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancelling stops future renewals; Fruition+ stays active until the current period ends.

Why can't I plant another goal?

The free tier is one goal at a time. Bring your current goal to Harvest to free your Plot and start a new one, or add Plots with Fruition+ to tend more at once.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your goals and history live on your device and in your own iCloud, not on our servers, and your evidence photos stay on your device. To coach you, the app sends the text you write to an AI service to generate your plan and reads. Full detail on the Privacy page.
