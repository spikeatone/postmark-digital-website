Building a constellation of visitor data for JTB

From five fields to a full portrait of every visitor

Today the Jamaica Tourist Board learns five things about each visitor when they enter the country. At this point, nearly every travel decision is already set in stone, and JTB only knows a sliver of information about the visitor.

Your Perfect Jamaica, running on Explorist™, builds a portrait of each person that starts the day they first imagine the trip and keeps growing long after they fly home.

1Where JTB starts

What landed info tells you, and what it can't

Immigration and customs give the Jamaica Tourist Board five fields for each visitor:

Zip code Length of stay Reason for travel Entry gateway Departure gateway

It's an accurate count of who arrived. It is also collected once, at the end of the decision, and it stops there. Landed info can't tell you:

  • who considered Jamaica and went somewhere else
  • what a visitor wanted from the trip, and whether they got it
  • where on the island they spent their days and their money
  • what they loved, and whether they'll come back

Those answers exist today. They sit with the airline, the booking site, and the resort. Your Perfect Jamaica gives the Jamaica Tourist Board its own source, gathered directly from each visitor.

2The constellation

How the portrait builds, stage by stage

Nobody fills out a survey. Every screen in the Perfect Jamaica app does something useful for the visitor, and every useful moment adds a signal. The profile behind it is our proprietary Seven Taste Axes, each with a confidence score, so the portrait gets sharper with every tap and knows where it is still guessing.

3Before they land

A portrait of the visitor before they ever set foot on the island

The research stage is where the app does something landed info never can: it knows potential visitors. A family in Chicago opens the Perfect Jamaica app in February, takes the 90-second interview, saves a guesthouse in Treasure Beach and a jerk pit in Boston Bay, and enters dates in June. The Jamaica Tourist Board now knows a great deal about four people who haven't even booked a flight.

26 profile-building signals before a single booking

Landed info arrives after the decision. The research stage gives JTB the portrait while the decision is still being made, when it can still make a difference.

Demand you can see coming

Which markets are dreaming about which months, and which Jamaica they want, weeks before bookings show up anywhere.

The visitors you're losing

People who entered dates, checked prices, and went quiet. Today they're invisible. Here they're an audience the app can reach with the thing they wanted most.

Campaigns aimed by taste

Instead of "U.S. Northeast, 35 to 54," reach slow-paced, local-leaning travelers who haven't picked their dates yet.

A first day that already fits

By the time they land, the app knows the party, the dates, the bookings, and the pace they like. Day one is planned before they leave home.

4The engine

Explorist turns the portrait into the right message, in real time

Explorist is our proprietary platform, built and proven, and already running for U.S. destination marketers and major casino resorts. It does two jobs. It keeps every visitor's portrait current as signals arrive, and it acts on that portrait for every visitor at once: what to recommend, what to say, and when to say it. It is the engine that makes every part of Your Perfect Jamaica work.

Recommendations

Matches each visitor to places on the Seven Taste Axes, then re-ranks for what's open now, the weather, travel time, and budget. Every recommendation carries a reason line.

Notifications written on the fly

Each push is composed for one person at the moment it's sent, from where they are, the time, the weather, and what's next in their day. "You're four minutes from a patty shop three locals just rated five stars. Detour?"

Email tuned to each visitor

Content, tone, and send time chosen per person and per stage: a countdown before the trip, the recap after it, and a reason to dream again a year later.

Discover and book in one conversation

Visitors ask what to do, get suggestions that fit them, and book without leaving the conversation. Every question they ask is signal too.

Stories told on location

History, food, and folklore narrated as visitors walk through Falmouth or along the Rio Grande. Which stories they finish, and where they linger, tells JTB what holds people.

Under the hood

Location comes from geofences around the island's places, so the app knows when someone arrives without draining their battery. Every signal updates the confidence score on the axis it touches: when confidence is low the app asks, and when it's high the app acts. One contact budget per visitor caps how often anyone hears from Jamaica, and it halves on its own after two ignored messages.

5Downstream uses

One constellation, put to work across the Jamaica Tourist Board

The app is where the data is gathered. Its value reaches every team at JTB, and every partner JTB markets with.

Marketing

  • Audiences built from taste and stage: dreamers, planners who stalled, past visitors due for a return.
  • Creative chosen by what each segment loves, so the slow-paced traveler sees Treasure Beach, not a party boat.
  • Media spend moved to the markets and months where intent is rising.
  • Campaigns measured by trips taken, not clicks.

Sales

  • Travel advisors and tour operators briefed with what their clients' markets want from Jamaica, and when they plan.
  • Airlift cases for airlines backed by intent by origin city, before the bookings exist.
  • Groups, weddings, and meetings: who travels together, how far ahead, and what they book on the island.
  • Sales calls that open with evidence instead of averages.

Co-op partners

  • Joint campaigns with airlines, hotels, and tour operators aimed at the visitors most likely to book with that partner.
  • Results reported in visits, nights, and spend, so every co-op dollar shows what it returned.
  • Shared insight reports that make JTB the partner with the best view of the visitor.
  • A stronger hand at the table: JTB brings an audience of its own, not only a budget.

Destination development

  • Which parishes visitors reach, which they never see, and whether the True Jamaica Team is changing that.
  • The experiences visitors look for and can't find: gaps worth filling with new products and events.
  • Evidence for where tourism spending should go next.

Local partners and small businesses

  • Views, saves, and bookings for every listing, reported back through Jamaica Connect.
  • Which places visitors loved, down to a single roadside corn soup stall.
  • What their likeliest visitors want, so they can shape what they offer.

Leadership and reporting

  • Incremental visits, on-island spend, length of stay, and return rate, tracked continuously.
  • Source-market trends weeks before arrival figures confirm them.
  • One account of the visitor, from first daydream to return trip, for the board and the Ministry.

Co-op partners, sales channels, and local businesses receive totals and trends. Individual profiles stay with the Jamaica Tourist Board (section 6).

Landed info becomes the baseline

Landed info doesn't go away. Compare app users with all arrivals from the same zip codes, and JTB can show what the app adds in length of stay, on-island spend, and return visits: the measures the proposal commits to, instead of downloads.

6Ownership and trust

JTB owns the data. Visitors stay in control of it.

  • The Jamaica Tourist Board owns every profile and every data point. Explorist processes it on JTB's behalf.
  • Location and notifications are opt-in, asked at the moment they're useful, never on first open.
  • Every visitor can see their profile, see why each recommendation appears, and correct it.
  • Partners and reports see totals and trends, never individuals.
  • Built to meet Jamaica's Data Protection Act, 2020, along with GDPR and CCPA for visitors from Europe and the U.S.

A visitor who understands why the app knows something keeps sharing. That trust is what keeps the portrait accurate.
