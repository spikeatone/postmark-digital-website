Pricing for Your Perfect Jamaica

Explorist, the foundational services layer, is already built and proven. So the price covers three things: the two apps and the site that connect to it, the platform that runs them, and the True Jamaica Team that finds the places no database lists.

The True Jamaica Team, per year$35,200 + transportation

Once a year, over three weeks, the team works its way around the island, two days in each of eight regions: Kingston, the Blue Mountains, Port Antonio, Ocho Rios, Discovery Bay, Montego Bay, Negril, and the South Coast. Each year's campaign refreshes the profiles already listed and signs up new vendors. Transportation will be priced once the route and vehicles are set.
