Pricing for Your Perfect Jamaica

Explorist, the foundational services layer, is already built and proven. So the price covers three things: the apps that connect to it, the platform that runs them, and the True Jamaica Team that finds the places no database lists.

1The line items

What's included, line by line

App design and development

Design and build of two apps that connect to Explorist:

  • The visitor app, for every visitor to Jamaica.
  • The True Jamaica Team sign-up app, which the team uses in the field to sign up small businesses on the spot.

The foundational services layer that drives the whole ecosystem, already built and proven. Two years is the minimum contract.

 $12,000per year
True Jamaica Team

Creation and daily management of the team of locals who find the island's overlooked small businesses and sign them up on the spot. Every profile still goes through JTB approval.

 $12,000per year
Jamaica Connect platform

The partner portal where businesses manage their listings and JTB approves what goes live.
