Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Short version: Golf Course Architect does not collect your personal information. There are no accounts, no advertising, and no tracking across other apps or websites. The app does send anonymous usage statistics and crash reports, and nothing in them can identify you. Your saved courses stay on your own device and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud storage.

Information we collect

No personal information. The app has no user accounts and no sign-in. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, or advertising identifiers, and the app does not track you across other apps or websites. There is no advertising SDK in Golf Course Architect and no IDFA, so you will never see a tracking permission prompt.

Anonymous usage statistics

To understand how far players get before they stop (the only way to tell whether the game is too slow, too hard, or asking to be paid for too early), the app sends a small number of anonymous events using TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service. These are counters and fixed categories only:

  • that a design company was founded: the event only, never the name you typed;
  • that a parcel of land was bought, its land type (links, desert, sandhills and so on) and whether it was prestige land: the category, never the parcel's name or country;
  • that a course broke ground, its category (municipal, daily fee, destination or prestige) and how many holes it has;
  • that a course opened for play, and which in-game day it opened on;
  • that the upgrade screen was shown, which of the two places in the game led there, and how many courses had been built by then;
  • whether that screen was closed or the full game was bought (never a price);
  • roughly how many minutes the app had been open.

What is never sent: the design company name you type, the names of parcels, courses, resorts or architects, your cash and finances, or any of the courses, routings, greens and terrain you design. All of that stays on your device.

TelemetryDeck is designed to be anonymous: it does not receive a name, an email address, or an advertising identifier, and it derives a rotating, one-way identifier that cannot be traced back to you or your device and is not shared with anyone. Apple classifies this as Product Interaction usage data, not linked to your identity and not used for tracking, which is how it is declared on the App Store.

Crash and performance reports

When the app crashes or freezes, iOS gives it a diagnostic report on a later launch. The app sends only a short summary of that report through TelemetryDeck so we can find and fix the problem: a code for the kind of crash, or how long a freeze lasted (under 3 seconds, 3 to 10 seconds, or longer), and the iOS version. It never sends the report itself, the app's code trail, any game content, or anything about you.

Game Center

If you are signed into Game Center, the achievements you earn and your leaderboard scores (your best course rating and your best ranking) are sent to Apple's Game Center under your Game Center profile, where your friends may see them. This is handled by Apple and covered by Apple's Privacy Policy; we only see the same leaderboards anyone can see.

Your game data

Saved courses are stored locally on your device, inside the app's own storage. They are never sent to us. There is no server and no account behind the game, so we cannot read, recover, or delete your saves.

The app also mirrors your saves to your own private iCloud key-value storage, so that a saved course survives reinstalling the app or moving to a new device. That copy belongs to your Apple Account, not to us: it is handled entirely by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy, we have no access to it, and it is not shared with anyone. If you are not signed in to iCloud, or you turn iCloud Drive off for this app, the game simply keeps your saves on the device and nothing is uploaded. Deleting the app deletes its local saves; if you back up your device, your saves are included in that backup, which Apple also handles.

Purchases

Golf Course Architect is free to play with one course. An optional one-time purchase unlocks the full game; there is no subscription. Payments are processed entirely by Apple; we never see or handle your payment details. We use RevenueCat to verify the purchase, which receives an anonymous identifier and the Apple purchase receipt in order to tell the app whether the full game is unlocked. It does not receive personal information from us.

Children

The app is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Third parties

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information, because we do not collect any. Three third parties are involved: Apple, which processes all payments and provides Game Center, iCloud and any device backup you choose to make; RevenueCat, which verifies the purchase; and TelemetryDeck, which receives the anonymous usage statistics and crash reports described above.
